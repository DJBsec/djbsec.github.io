## Mirage2FA Phishing Kit Hijacks Microsoft 365 Sessions Across 3,500 Organizations

<span class="badge badge-primary" style="font-size: 0.7rem;">Phishing</span>

A phishing-as-a-service toolkit called Mirage2FA has been linked to attacks targeting 3,518 organizations and the potential compromise of more than 4,500 Microsoft 365 accounts. The kit uses adversary-in-the-middle phishing pages to relay usernames, passwords, and MFA codes to Microsoft in real time before stealing the authenticated session cookie generated after a successful login. Researchers observed thousands of compromise events across 94 countries, with U.S. organizations accounting for nearly two-thirds of identified victims and technology, manufacturing, and education among the most affected sectors. Because the attackers obtain valid session cookies, traditional MFA and a simple password reset may not be enough to immediately terminate unauthorized access. Organizations should prioritize phishing-resistant authentication such as FIDO2 or passkeys and revoke active sessions and tokens when an account is suspected of being compromised.
